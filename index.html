The Problem

Projects Run on Chat Threads, Paper and Memory

Scattered Documents

Drawings, quotes, invoices and approvals live in chat groups, email and phone galleries. Finding the right version costs hours every week.

Admin After a Long Day

Daily logs, RFIs, change orders and owner updates are written at night, late or not at all. Small builders have no project coordinator.

Handover Takes Weeks

Manuals, warranties, serial numbers and trade contacts are gathered by hand at the end of the job, and the owner still calls the builder for every question.

The Product

Two Tools, One Record of the Building

SiteAgent starts with the work builders already do and already pay for. Every action adds to a structured Home Record that stays with the building.

Builder Workspace

Run the Job

  • Projects, team roles and client access
  • Upload anything; files are classified and linked automatically
  • Ask the project: answers cite the drawing, page or invoice
  • Daily logs from voice notes or text
  • RFIs, change orders and weekly client updates drafted for approval
Handover Generator

Hand Over the Home

  • Reads closeout files: manuals, warranties, invoices, photos
  • Extracts equipment, model and serial numbers, dates and trades
  • Builds a branded handover packet in hours, not weeks
  • Publishes an owner app: who to call, shutoffs, systems, maintenance calendar
  • Tracks warranty dates and reminds the owner

A person approves every output. SiteAgent does not produce stamped drawings or replace licensed architects and engineers.

Home Record Schema

One data model shared from design to ownership. Each project adds structured, reusable data about real buildings.

Document Pipeline

Turns messy uploads into records: document type, equipment, model and serial numbers, dates, parties and amounts.

Cost Engine

Regional unit costs by finish tier, so changes and estimates are priced from real numbers rather than guesses.

Construction Agents

Task-specific agents with human approval, written for builders' real workflows, not generic chat.

Evaluation Suite

Test sets from real project paperwork measure accuracy on every release, so quality goes up, not sideways.

Model-Agnostic Layer

No lock-in to one AI provider. Models can be switched as prices fall and quality rises.
