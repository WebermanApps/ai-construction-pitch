The Problem

Sites Run on Chat Threads, Paper and Memory

Small and mid-size contractors do most of the region's building, but they're the least served by software. The admin falls on the site manager after a 10-hour day.

Lost in the Chat

Instructions, photos and approvals are scattered across messaging groups. Nothing is filed, and nothing is searchable when a dispute starts.

Safety in Translation

Multilingual crews from Thailand, Myanmar and Cambodia. Studies by the ILO and others find that language barriers weaken safety training and accident reporting.

Slow Paper, Slow Cash

Late daily logs, RFIs nobody chases, and client updates that never go out. The result is delays, variation disputes and progress billing weeks behind the work.

The Solution

Seven Agents. One Assistant. Zero Extra Admin.

Each agent does one job end to end: it reads the site's inputs, drafts the document, files it and follows up. Your team taps to approve.

Why Now · Why Us

The Tech Is Ready. The Builders Are Waiting.

Why Now

  • AI agents now do real work. Leaders like Procore already ship agents for daily logs and RFIs, but for enterprise users and in English.
  • Labour is tight. Skilled-labour shortage is the top restraint on Thai construction (Mordor Intelligence).
  • Documentation pressure is rising. BIM in public tenders and stricter quality rules for state work reward contractors with good records.
  • The long tail is still analogue. 83% of Thai construction value still uses conventional methods.

Why Us

  • Builder-founder. Max runs construction projects and rental properties, and lives this problem every week.
  • Already working. The first construction agent was built for and used on his own projects.
  • Thai-first by design. Language, workflows and integrations built in Thailand for Thai and SEA SMEs.
  • Human in the loop. Nothing goes to a client, engineer or ledger without a tap to approve.
